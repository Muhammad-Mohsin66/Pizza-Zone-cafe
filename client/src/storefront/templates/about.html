Welcome to Pizza Zone & Cafe

Pizza Zone & Cafe is your premier destination for mouth-watering pizzas, burgers, shawarmas, wraps, and beverages. Located at ADA SHREENWALA MOR, LAHORE - MULTAN ROAD, SAHIWAL, we serve freshly prepared food crafted with passion and premium ingredients.

From our signature Zone Special Crown Crust Pizza loaded with cheese and chicken to crispy Zinger Burgers, flame-grilled burgers, and refreshing Margaritas, we promise taste that wows every single time.

We offer Free Home Delivery (1.5 KM, Minimum Order Rs. 600/-)! Call or WhatsApp us on 0309-7150999 or 0310-7150999 to place your order.
